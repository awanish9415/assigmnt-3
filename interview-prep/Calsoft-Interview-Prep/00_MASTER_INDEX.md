## Interview Tips

1. **Speak slowly and clearly** - Don't rush.
2. **If you don't know, say so** - "I have not worked on this directly, but my understanding is..."
3. **Give real examples** - Mention what you have done in projects.
4. **Draw diagrams** - Ask if you can share screen and draw on paper.
5. **Ask questions back** - Shows interest.
6. **Stay calm** - Take a breath before answering.

Good luck! You can do this! 🚀
