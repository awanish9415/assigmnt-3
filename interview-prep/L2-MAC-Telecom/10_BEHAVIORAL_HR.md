# 🎯 Behavioral & HR Round Questions

## 🎯 STAR Method (For Answering)

**S** - Situation (Context)  
**T** - Task (What to do)  
**A** - Action (What you did)  
**R** - Result (Outcome)

## 🎯 Salary Negotiation Tips

### **Research Phase:**

**Tools:**
- Glassdoor.com
- AmbitionBox.in
- LinkedIn Salary
- PayScale

**Data Points:**
- 5 years L2/MAC engineer Bangalore: ₹15-30 LPA
- L2/MAC senior: ₹25-45 LPA
- Architect level: ₹40-70 LPA

### **Negotiation Phases:**

**Phase 1: First Quote**
"Based on my experience and market research, I'm looking at ₹22-28 LPA"

**Phase 2: Counter-offer**
- If they offer ₹18 LPA
- "I appreciate the offer. Given my 5 years of specific L2/MAC experience and the role demands, I was expecting ₹22-25 LPA. Can we discuss?"

**Phase 3: Negotiation**
- Ask for breakdown:
  - Base: ?
  - Variable: ?
  - RSU/ESOP: ?
  - Joining bonus: ?
- Negotiate weakest component

**Phase 4: Closing**
- Get offer in writing
- Don't accept on phone
- Read all details
- Negotiate joining date

### **Beyond Salary:**

✅ Sign-on bonus  
✅ Annual bonus structure  
✅ Stock options  
✅ Insurance coverage  
✅ Notice period  
✅ Work hours flexibility  
✅ Remote work option  
✅ Learning budget  
✅ Conference attendance  
✅ Designation
